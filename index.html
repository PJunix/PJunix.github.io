Approach

Learning by testing, observing, and documenting.

PJunix is a growing security research practice focused on practical testing and clear technical notes. The work starts with vulnerable labs and machines, moves through manual web application testing, and follows evidence into Linux enumeration and privilege escalation.

Rather than treating a tool output as an answer, each experiment is an opportunity to understand behavior, validate assumptions, and record a repeatable process.
